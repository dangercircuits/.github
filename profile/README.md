# Danger Circuits

**BE AWARE: WE MAKE THINGS WORK.**

Old-school hacking across code, sound, light and hardware. Useful(less) experiments.

We build things because they need to exist, because they solve a problem, or simply because we want to understand what happens when they do.

Sometimes three lines are enough. Sometimes they aren't even close.

## Projects

**[Mad Photons](https://madphotons.com)**  
Light direction and automation for DJs, parties and small shows.

**[Pepperizer](https://pepperizer.me)**  
AI agents with persistent Linux workspaces, accessible through WhatsApp.

*In development:* Paiol DMX and Beat Detector. Names and release dates are not final.

## From the lab

**[Auri EQ for Android](https://dangercircuits.com/labs/auri/)**  
An unofficial Android adaptation of Kuba Audio's original Auri web equalizer. The original presets and interface, with the missing USB bridge.
